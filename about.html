About the Chamber

Grounded in tax law. Committed to people.

ADA Law Chamber is a specialised law firm with deep expertise in Goods and Services Tax (GST) and direct tax matters. We provide focused, practical, and strategic legal solutions in all aspects of GST law, from compliance and advisory to litigation and dispute resolution.

We provide strategic legal solutions in FEMA, Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, arbitration, direct and indirect taxation, mergers and acquisitions, contract drafting and negotiation, and the entire spectrum of corporate laws.

With sound expertise in civil and criminal litigation, we support our clients at every stage — from preventive advisory to dispute resolution. Our approach combines deep technical knowledge with practical insight to deliver clear, effective, and result-oriented solutions.
