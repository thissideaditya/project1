The Chamber

Our Team

Our team is led by Adv. Ashu Dalmia, who has extensive experience and deep specialisation in GST law. Under his guidance, the chamber consists of dedicated associates who focus on delivering precise and effective solutions in tax laws and corporate matters.

Adv. Ashu Dalmia

Founder · CHARTED ACCOUNTANT & ADVOCATE

Ashu Dalmia founded the Chambers on the strength of over two decades of practice at the intersection of taxation, GST consultancy, and litigation. A rank holder both at graduation from Lucknow University and in CA Inter, he built his early reputation on rigorous, evidence-led advisory work — a discipline that continues to define his approach to indirect taxation, transfer pricing, audits, valuations, and corporate governance today. His client base spans Indian and foreign corporates, with particular depth in the logistics and services sectors, where he is regularly sought out for structuring and dispute strategy alike. His practice carries a genuine international dimension: he has led VAT implementation projects in Saudi Arabia and served as Master Trainer for UAE VAT, giving him a comparative vantage point on indirect tax regimes that few Indian practitioners bring to a GST mandate. In India, that standing was formally recognised when he was named a Special Invitee to the Indirect Tax Committee of ICAI (2018–19), and he remains active as ICAI faculty, training professionals on GST in India and VAT in the UAE. He is also a prolific author and speaker — co-author of three books on GST published by Taxmann, Wolters Kluwer, and LMP, and a presenter at more than 1,000 workshops and training sessions for forums including ICAI, ASSOCHAM, CII, and the PHD Chamber of Commerce, in India and abroad. His credentials extend into forensic and information-systems territory as well, with DISA (ICAI) and CISA (ISACA, USA) qualifications alongside specialised certificate courses in forensic accounting, fraud detection, arbitration, and bank audits. More recently, he completed the Leadership 4.0 programme under Prof. Debashis Chatterjee at IIM Kozhikode, an AI course at IIT Mumbai, and Independent Director training from the IICA

Smita Raj

Tax Professional

Smita Raj is a law graduate and tax professional who brings over 16 years of experience in taxation, finance, and indirect tax advisory to the Chambers. Since the implementation of GST in India, she has worked closely with businesses navigating the resulting regulatory frameworks — not merely on paper compliance, but through the harder work of handling departmental assessments and notices, and representing clients in litigation, always with a practical, solution-oriented approach that keeps the client's operational reality in view. Her advisory range extends well beyond GST itself: direct and indirect taxation, company incorporation, payroll management, and Provident Fund compliances all fall within her remit, alongside the design of business process systems that businesses rely on day to day. That breadth allows her to serve as a single point of reference for clients whose compliance needs cut across several regulatory regimes at once. Alongside her advisory practice, she has built and led teams, streamlined operational processes, and taken on resource planning and administrative management — experience that shows in how she structures client engagements. She is known for professionalism, client satisfaction, and staying current with regulatory change, and for an underlying philosophy of building long-term professional relationships through integrity, efficiency, and value-driven consultancy.

Adv. Samiksha Goswami

Legal PRACTITIONER

Ankita Jha is a young, dynamic, and hardworking legal practitioner whose practice centres on GST matters, civil litigation, insurance disputes, and corporate compliance. Across these areas she has built genuine drafting fluency — GST replies, appeals, and GSTAT filings, alongside pleadings and other legal documents — and applies the same precision to insurance claims and MACT cases, where the stakes for individual clients are often high and personal. She is a committed proponent of Alternative Dispute Resolution, actively steering matters toward amicable settlement through mediation and arbitration wherever that route serves the client better than protracted litigation. This inclination toward resolution, paired with sharp legal acumen, meticulous drafting, and strong advocacy where a matter does need to be contested, gives her practice an unusual range for someone still early in her career. Colleagues and clients describe her as bringing energy, dedication, and a client-first approach to every matter she handles, with a stated commitment to delivering practical and effective legal solutions while holding to standards of excellence, integrity, and professionalism.

V A R & Associates

V A R & Associates is a firm of Chartered Accountants established on 20 October 1997 and based in Delhi — nearly three decades of continuous practice in a field that has changed considerably around it, most notably with the introduction of GST itself. Its work today centres on GST advisory, consultancy, and litigation, alongside audit and assurance and routine compliance. The firm's more specialised offerings — GST health checks and reviews, and input tax credit and refund work — speak to a practice that has stayed close to the technical detail of GST as it has evolved, rather than treating it as settled once implemented.
