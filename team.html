Our Team

Our team is led by Adv. Ashu Dalmia, who has extensive experience and deep specialisation in GST law. Under his guidance, the chamber consists of dedicated associates who focus on delivering precise and effective solutions in tax laws and corporate matters.

Founder CA & Advocate

Adv. Ashu Dalmia

Founder · Chartered Accountant & Advocate

Ashu Dalmia founded the Chambers on the strength of over two decades of practice at the intersection of taxation, GST consultancy, and litigation. A rank holder both at graduation from Lucknow University and in CA Inter, he built his early reputation on rigorous, evidence-led advisory work — a discipline that continues to define his approach to indirect taxation, transfer pricing, audits, valuations, and corporate governance today. His client base spans Indian and foreign corporates, with particular depth in the logistics and services sectors.

Her advisory range extends to direct and indirect taxation, company incorporation, payroll management, Provident Fund compliances, and the design of business process systems.

  • 16+ years in taxation, finance, and indirect tax advisory
  • GST departmental assessments, notices, and litigation representation
  • Direct and indirect taxation, company incorporation, payroll, and PF compliance
  • Business process system design for operational efficiency
